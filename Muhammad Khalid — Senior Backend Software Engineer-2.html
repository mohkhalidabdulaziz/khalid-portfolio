Practical tips
  • Quantify your impactLead with outcomes such as latency, cost or volume, and be ready to explain how you measured them.
  • Know the domainFor banking and fintech roles, revisit transactions, consistency, idempotency and failure handling.
  • Clarify before designingIn system design rounds, ask about scale, constraints and requirements before drawing boxes.
  • Prepare for several roundsExpect a mix of HR, technical and hiring-manager conversations, and prepare a different focus for each.
  • Have your numbers readyBe clear on your notice period, expected salary in MYR and earliest start date.
  • Explain your decisionsInterviewers value the reasoning and trade-offs behind a solution as much as the solution itself.
